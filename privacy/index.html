Privacy policy

Last updated 28 September 2026 · Version 2

AMBO ("AMBO", the "App", "we", "us") is a mobile app that records your boxing sessions on your phone, analyses your movement, and gives you feedback and drills. This policy covers the App, the amboapp.ai website and the AMBO early access list. It explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

The short version. The App records video and audio of you training and analyses your movement on your device. We upload that recording and the movement data from it to our cloud and use it to improve and train the models that power the App. Your account is identified by your email address. We don't ask for your name or phone number, we don't sell your data and we don't run ads. You can ask us to delete everything at any time.

1. Who is responsible for your data

AMBO is provided by Mathe Wieme, an individual based in the United Arab Emirates, who is the controller of the personal data described here. Questions or requests: support@amboapp.ai.

2. What we collect

Early access list (website)

When you join early access on amboapp.ai we collect your email address, the campaign or referral tag in the link you used (for example a TikTok link), and basic email delivery data (whether our emails were delivered, opened or clicked). The website does not use advertising cookies or trackers.

Session recordings (App)

When you record a session, the App captures video and audio of you training using your device's camera and microphone. The audio includes the sound of your strikes, which helps the App detect and time your punches.

Movement and analysis data

From each recording the App derives body-position ("pose" or "skeleton") data, on-device analysis results (such as punch counts and form metrics) and, in guided sessions, a timeline of the drills and cues that were called out.

Account and identifiers

A login is required to use the App. You can sign in with Google, Apple, or an email address and password. However you sign in, we collect your email address and assign a user identifier so your data can be stored and synced across sessions and devices. We do not collect your name or phone number. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your email address from that provider but never your password. If you use an email and password, the password is handled by our authentication provider and stored only in hashed form.

Preferences and consent

The App stores the preferences you choose during setup (such as your stance, experience and goal) and a record that you accepted this policy, including the date and version.

Technical data

Our service providers process standard technical information needed to operate the App and website, such as app version, device type, network requests and timestamps.

3. How we use your data

  • To run the App: recording sessions and analysing your movement. Most analysis happens on your device.
  • To store and sync your sessions so they are available to you.
  • To improve and train our machine-learning models. We use your uploaded recordings and the movement data derived from them to develop, evaluate and retrain the models that power AMBO's analysis and feedback.
  • To run early access: confirming your spot, sending your launch offer and occasional updates about AMBO, and understanding which channels bring people in. This is based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by unsubscribing.
  • To maintain, secure and debug the service.

We do not use your data for third-party advertising, and we do not sell your personal data.

4. On-device processing

AMBO's movement analysis runs on your phone. Uploading your recordings and derived data to our cloud is what enables storage, sync and the model-improvement use described above.

5. Who we share data with

We use service providers to host and process data on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Realtime Database, Cloud Storage) for App accounts and data, governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Loops to store the early access list and send our emails.
  • Vercel to host the amboapp.ai website.

We share data with service providers only as needed to run AMBO, and we may disclose data where required by law.

6. International transfers

We are based in the United Arab Emirates, and our service providers may store and process your data in other countries, including the United States. Where data is transferred internationally, we rely on our providers' safeguards for protecting it.

7. How long we keep your data

We keep App recordings and derived data for as long as needed to provide the App and to develop and improve our models, unless you ask us to delete them. We keep your early access email until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. If AMBO does not launch, we delete the list.

8. Your rights and choices

You can unsubscribe from emails with the link in any email. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, or to stop using it to improve our models, by emailing support@amboapp.ai. Please contact us from the email address linked to your account or signup so we can find your data.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021), the EU/UK GDPR, or the California Consumer Privacy Act. We honour these rights where they apply to you. If you are in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, including the security features of our cloud providers, to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children

AMBO is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has used AMBO, contact us and we will delete the data.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy and will change the date at the top when we do. If we make a material change to how we handle App data, we will ask you to review and accept the updated policy in the App before you continue recording.
